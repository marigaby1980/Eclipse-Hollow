ECLIPSE

H O L L O W

They are faster than you. You can see all of them coming. Nothing hides here — the field is lit and open, and that is the problem. Grab the world with your hands and swing. Every hard release stacks momentum and raises your ceiling. Stop swinging for two seconds and you are back inside their reach.

Objective: carry FUSES out of the field, feed the generator in the shelter, then run the north tunnel. Powering it kills every light for three seconds and wakes everything at once.
Lobby: a terminal stands in the shelter. Type a room code on its keys with your hands, then HOST or JOIN.
If you bleed out you come back as one of them, and you hunt the people you came in with.
